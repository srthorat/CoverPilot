# What the yellow step-gate messages mean and how to fix them

## Message: Finish this step before moving on
This yellow box appears when you try to open a later step while something on the current step is missing or invalid. The bullet list under it is exactly what is left to do. Fix each item and press Continue again. The app also moves the cursor to the first missing field.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Enter your full legal name
The name box is empty or too short. Type your name as printed on your PAN or Aadhaar card, or say it and the assistant will type it.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Enter your date of birth as DD/MM/YYYY
The date of birth is missing or not a real date. Use day, month and year with slashes, for example 05/03/1992. A month above twelve or a day that does not exist is refused.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: This demo product covers ages 18 to 65
The date of birth is valid but gives an age below eighteen or above sixty-five. In this demo product only those ages can apply. Check that you did not mistype the year. This age band is a NexaSure demo limit, not a legal rule.
Source: NexaSure demo product limits (not a regulation).

## Message: Choose your gender
Neither Male nor Female is selected. Press one of the two buttons, or tell the assistant.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Enter your annual income
No income range is chosen. Open the dropdown and pick the range your yearly income falls in, or tell the assistant your income and it will choose the range.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Say whether you use tobacco
The tobacco question has not been answered. Press Non-Smoker or Smoker, or tell the assistant. The button does not look selected until you choose.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Enter your city
The city box is empty. Type the city you live in or say it.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Enter your 6-digit PIN code
The PIN is missing or is not exactly six digits. Digits only, no spaces. Say it digit by digit and the assistant will type it.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Enter your 12-digit Aadhaar number
The Aadhaar box in identity records does not hold twelve digits. Type your number yourself, or scan the sample Aadhaar. The assistant cannot see or fill this box, so do not say the number aloud.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Enter your PAN like ABCDE1234F
The PAN box does not match five letters, four digits and one letter. Type it in capitals without spaces. The assistant cannot see or fill this box.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Enter your 10-digit mobile number
The mobile number must be ten digits and start with six, seven, eight or nine. Type it yourself in the identity records box; the assistant cannot see it.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Confirm or undo what the agent entered
The assistant filled in your income, smoking status or nominees and is waiting for your click. Press Confirm to keep the value or Undo to restore the earlier one. Saying yes aloud does not clear it.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: A value differs, you typed one thing and the document shows another
A scanned document disagreed with what you typed, for example the name or date of birth. Press Keep mine or Use the document's on the amber card. Nothing is changed until you choose, and the step stays blocked until then.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Choose at least one rider, or tick I do not want any riders
Step two needs an explicit decision on riders. Tick a rider card, or tick the box that says you do not want any riders, then continue.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Name your primary nominee, second nominee or every additional nominee
A nominee row has no name. Give every nominee you have added a name, a relation and a share, or remove the extra rows you do not need.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Nominee shares must total 100 percent
The shares of all nominees do not add up to one hundred. The message shows the current total. Change the shares, or ask the assistant to split them equally, until they total exactly one hundred.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: A nominee under 18 needs an adult appointee
At least one nominee is under eighteen and no appointee is named. Enter the name and relation of an adult who will receive the money for the child while the child is a minor.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour; appointee rule: Insurance Act 1938 section 39, https://lawgist.in/insurance-act/39

## Message: Tick the Section 45 declaration
Step four cannot be left until you tick the declaration yourself. Read it first. The assistant explains it but never ticks it for you.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Message: Not signed yet or Not paid yet on the application check
On step five, Not signed yet means you have not typed your name and ticked the declaration in the e-sign card, and Not paid yet means the demo payment is still to be done. Both are yours to complete, and the Confirm and Lock button stays disabled until both are done.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).
