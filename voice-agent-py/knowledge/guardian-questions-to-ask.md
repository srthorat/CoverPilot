# Questions to put to the insurer

## Questions about the premium and the cover
Ask: Can you confirm in writing the sum assured, the policy term and the premium shown on my schedule? Ask: Is the premium fixed for the whole term, or can it be revised? Ask: Why does the premium on the document differ from the one in my application? Ask: Does the premium include the riders I chose, and what does each rider cost?
Source: NexaSure Guardian suggested questions (not a regulation).

## Questions about grace, lapse and revival
Ask: How many days of grace do I get for my payment mode? Ask: What exactly happens to my cover if I miss a payment by a few days or by a month? Ask: How long can I revive a lapsed policy, and what interest or late fee will I pay? Ask: Will I need a medical examination or a health declaration to revive it?
Source: NexaSure Guardian suggested questions (not a regulation); IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, section III, para 3.

## Questions about nomination
Ask: Where in my policy are my nominees and their shares recorded? Ask: How do I add a nominee or change the shares later, and how long does the update take? Ask: If my nominee is a minor, how is the appointee recorded and who receives the money? Ask: Will my nomination stay valid if I assign the policy to a lender?
Source: NexaSure Guardian suggested questions (not a regulation); IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, section IV, para 3.

## Questions about riders
Ask: Is the rider I chose included in my policy, and where is it listed? Ask: What is the waiting period and the survival period for the critical illness rider? Ask: Which illnesses and events are covered, and which are excluded? Ask: Does the critical illness rider use the standard IRDAI wording for each illness, and if not, how does the wording differ? Ask: What happens to the riders if I stop paying the base premium?
Source: NexaSure Guardian suggested questions (not a regulation).

## Questions about exclusions and limits
Ask: Please list every exclusion that applies to my policy and its riders. Ask: Does the suicide clause follow the twelve-month rule, and what does the nominee receive in that period? Ask: Are there exclusions for my occupation, hobbies or travel that I should know about? Ask: Are the exclusions in the policy wording the same as those in the Customer Information Sheet?
Source: NexaSure Guardian suggested questions (not a regulation); IRDAI Master Circular on Life Insurance Products 2024, para 21.

## Questions about claims
Ask: Which documents will my nominee need to make a claim, and where are they listed? Ask: How can a claim be reported, and to whom? Ask: How long will you take to settle a death claim, and when do you pay interest for delay? Ask: If you ever want to reject a claim, will you give the reasons and the clause in writing?
Source: NexaSure Guardian suggested questions (not a regulation); IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, section V.

## Questions about free-look and cancelling
Ask: How many days do I have to review this policy and cancel, counted from when? Ask: What will be deducted from my refund if I cancel in that period? Ask: How do I send my free-look request, and how quickly will the refund reach me?
Source: NexaSure Guardian suggested questions (not a regulation); IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, section III, para 2.

## Questions about complaints and changes
Ask: Who is the grievance officer for my policy and how do I reach them? Ask: Which Insurance Ombudsman office covers my area? Ask: How quickly will you update my address, contact details or nominee after I ask?
Source: NexaSure Guardian suggested questions (not a regulation); IRDAI Master Circular on Life Insurance Products 2024, para 13.

## Questions when the document and your application disagree
Ask: The document shows a different figure from my application. Which one will the policy use? Ask: Can you issue a corrected document before I pay, and will I get a new Customer Information Sheet? Ask: If I correct the application, will the premium or cover change?
Source: NexaSure Guardian suggested questions (not a regulation).
