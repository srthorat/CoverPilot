# Claims, grievances and the insurance ombudsman

## Documents for a death claim
The nominee sends the claim form, the original policy or its details, the death certificate, the nominee's ID and bank proof. If the death was unnatural, a police report and post-mortem may be needed. The insurer may ask for more, but cannot reject a claim only because some documents are missing.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, life section, "At the time of claim".

## How long a claim takes
IRDAI requires a death claim to be settled within fifteen days of getting all documents. If the insurer needs to investigate, it must finish within forty-five days. If it pays late, it owes interest at the bank rate plus two percent. NexaSure's two-hour response is a demo target.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, life section, claim timelines.

## Why claims get rejected
The main reasons are a wrong or hidden fact in the proposal within the first three years, a lapsed policy, and a death within an excluded period. After three years a claim can be rejected only for proven fraud. The insurer must give its reasons in writing.
Source: Insurance Act 1938 section 45; IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024.

## Making a complaint
First write to the insurer's grievance officer. The insurer must acknowledge in a few days and resolve it within fourteen days. If you are not satisfied, escalate to IRDAI through its grievance portal, Bima Bharosa, or call its toll-free helpline. Keep the complaint number safe.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, grievance redressal; IRDAI Bima Bharosa portal.

## Insurance ombudsman
If the insurer rejects your complaint or does not answer within thirty days, you can go to the Insurance Ombudsman, free of charge, within one year. It can order the insurer to pay claims within a monetary limit set by the Ombudsman Rules. You do not need a lawyer. You can also go to a consumer court.
Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules 2017; IRDAI Ombudsman Rules FAQ.

## Unclaimed amounts
If a claim or refund stays unpaid, the insurer must still hold it and show it on its website. Nominees and policyholders can claim it later by proving identity. Sums unclaimed for a long time are moved to a senior citizens' welfare fund.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, unclaimed amounts para.
