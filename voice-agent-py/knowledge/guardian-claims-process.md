# How a claim works and where to complain

## How to tell the insurer about a claim: report a death and register the claim
The claimant should tell the insurer as early as possible, in person or through an online mode, the distribution channel, the insurer's authorised call centre, or any other way the policy document specifies. A claim cannot be rejected or closed because some documents are missing or because it was reported late.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, section V, para 1.

## Documents the insurer must list
For a death claim the documents usually include the claim form and the death certificate, and the first information report, post mortem report or hospital discharge summary where they apply. The insurer must state the documents it needs in the policy document and show the same on its website. If your document does not list them, ask for the list.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, section V, para 2(3).

## How long the insurer has to settle a death claim
A death claim must be settled within fifteen days of intimation, or within forty-five days if it needs investigation. Surrender or partial withdrawal is due within seven days of the request, and maturity or survival benefits on their due date.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, section V, para 2(1).

## Interest when a claim is late
If the claim is not settled within these timelines, the claimant is entitled to interest at the bank rate plus two percent, counted from the date the insurer received the intimation until the date of payment. The insurer must pay this on its own along with the claim amount, without being asked.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, section V, para 2(2).

## When the policy has been assigned to a lender
If the policy was assigned, for example to a bank for a loan, the nominee should check whether the assignment is valid and what is still owed. The insurer pays the lender only the outstanding dues, and any balance of the death benefit goes directly to the nominee's bank account.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, section V, para 2(4).

## What a rejection has to rest on
No claim may be repudiated without legally tenable evidence, and none may be rejected or closed for want of documents or late intimation. Within the first three years the insurer can question a policy only for fraud or a wrong or hidden fact that matters, and the Act says it must give the insured, or the legal representatives, nominees or assignees, the grounds and materials in writing. If it repudiates for a misstatement and not fraud, it must return the premiums collected. The rule to cite the specific policy terms is written for health claims, so for a life claim ask the insurer to point to the clause it relies on. The Guardian explains these rules but never says how a particular claim will be decided.
Source: Insurance Act 1938 section 45(2) and (4) and their provisos, read in the Gazette copy of the Insurance Laws (Amendment) Act 2015; IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, section IV para 6 and section V para 1(ii) (life), and the health claims paragraph 5 (health only).

## Taking a complaint through the steps
Start with the insurer's grievance officer. A complaint should be acknowledged immediately and the insurer should give a resolution within fourteen days. If you are not satisfied, you can escalate to IRDAI through the Bima Bharosa portal, and you can go to the Insurance Ombudsman, or to a consumer court.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, section VI.

## Going to the Insurance Ombudsman
Complain to the insurer in writing first. You can go to the Ombudsman if the insurer rejects the complaint, does not reply within one month, or you are not satisfied, and you must do so within one year. It is free and you do not need a lawyer. The Ombudsman passes an award within three months, the award is capped at fifty lakh rupees, and the insurer must comply within thirty days. If it does not honour the award, it owes five thousand rupees a day to the complainant.
Source: Insurance Ombudsman Rules 2017, rules 14 and 17 (limit raised to fifty lakh in November 2023); IRDAI Master Circular on Life Insurance Products 2024, para 14.

## Changing nominee or contact details later
When you ask the insurer to update details such as your address, contact details or nominee, it must acknowledge the request immediately and update it within seven days. If it does not, you can register a grievance.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, section IV, para 3.
