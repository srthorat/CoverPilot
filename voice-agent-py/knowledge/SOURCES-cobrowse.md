# VERIFIED: KYC methods and PAN or Form 60. IRDAI Master Guidelines on AML/CFT, 1 Aug 2022 (https://irdai.gov.in, PDF read in full): para 8.1.6 (verify identity, address and recent photograph; documents per sub-rules 4 and 18 of rule 9 of the PML Rules; Aadhaar not mandatory, officially valid documents allowed instead), para 8.1.7 (methods: Aadhaar online authentication, Aadhaar offline verification, digital KYC, video based identification, KYC identifier from CKYCR, officially valid documents, and PAN/Form 60 wherever applicable), para 8.2.2 (existing customers with aggregate premium of not more than 50,000 rupees in a financial year: PAN/Form 60 by a date to be notified), para 12.7 (only the last four digits of Aadhaar go to CKYCR), Annexure II paras a and p (video identification, Aadhaar number to be redacted).
# VERIFIED: officially valid documents. PML (Maintenance of Records) Rules 2005, rule 2(1)(d) and rule 9(4), (15), (18), read on https://fiuindia.gov.in/files/AML_Legislation/notification.html (consolidated copy of the rules; the Gazette governs and later amendments may apply). Rule 9(4)(b): PAN or Form 60 is asked in addition to the Aadhaar or officially valid document; PAN is not in the rule 2(1)(d) list.
# VERIFIED: smoker and non-smoker are not defined by IRDAI in the Master Circular on Life Insurance Products, 12 Jun 2024 (https://irdai.gov.in, 69-page PDF read): the words appear only in the product filing form (item 6.1(b), a tick box) and the underwriting section (item 11(b), a class), never with a definition. This covers that circular only, not every IRDAI document. Look-back periods and cotinine testing are insurer practice from insurer pages and secondary articles, not a rule.
