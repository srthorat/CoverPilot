# Frequently asked questions

## Why does the form ask for my annual income
The insurer uses your income to judge how much cover is reasonable and whether you can keep paying the premium. It may ask for proof such as a salary slip, Form 16 or an income tax return. There is no IRDAI formula for cover as a multiple of income; any multiple is the insurer's own underwriting practice.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, life section, proposal form para iv, https://www.caalley.com/irdai_mc/MC_Protection_of_Policyholders_interests_2024.pdf

## Why does the form ask for my date of birth and gender
Premium is priced by age and gender when you buy. In this demo product the rate stays fixed for the thirty-year term. Age proof such as Aadhaar or PAN is needed, so the date of birth must match your document exactly.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, life section, KYC para v; rate lock is a NexaSure demo term.

## What happens if I miss a premium
You get a grace period of thirty days, or fifteen days for monthly premiums, and the policy stays fully in force. If you still have not paid, the policy lapses and cover stops. You can revive it during the period in your policy document by paying the dues with interest or a late fee. In this demo product that period is five years.
Source: IRDAI Master Circulars on Life Insurance Products (para 8) and Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024; five years is a NexaSure demo term.

## How are claims filed
The nominee tells the insurer about the death online, in person, through the distribution channel or by phone, and then sends the claim form with the death certificate and other documents. The insurer cannot reject or close a claim only because documents are missing or the intimation was late. NexaSure's two-hour digital response is a demo target, not an audited result.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, life section, "At the time of claim".

## Can I change my nominee later
Yes. A nomination can be changed at any time during the policy term, by a written endorsement the insurer registers. The exception is a policy under the Married Women's Property Act trust, where the beneficiaries are fixed.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Life Insurance Products 2024, para 6; Insurance Act 1938 section 39(2), https://lawgist.in/insurance-act/39

## Can I increase my cover later
In this demo product the life stage escalator raises cover on marriage or the birth of a child without a new medical test. Otherwise you would need a new proposal with fresh underwriting.
Source: NexaSure demo product terms (not a regulation).

## What is co-browsing and what does the agent see
The agent sees your live form answers, such as name, date of birth and income, and a reduced-size picture of the form when you change step. Aadhaar, PAN and mobile numbers typed in the identity fields are not sent and are masked on screen. If you scan a document, photos, signatures, QR codes and ID numbers are hidden on your device first, but your name and date of birth stay readable so the form can be filled.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## What are the sample documents
The three sample buttons load demo documents: a PAN card, an Aadhaar card and a salary slip. They are fictional. Press a button and the app hides the private parts in your browser before the reading model sees the card, then reads the name and date of birth, or the income from the slip, and fills those fields. You can also type everything yourself, and you can enter your own identity numbers in the identity fields instead.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Who sees my identity documents
In this demo only the three fictional sample cards are used, and there is no upload, so no real document leaves your device. For a sample, Meta's SAM model is shown the original card to find the photo, signature, QR code and Aadhaar number, and a second Meta model finds the PAN, UAN and bank account number on blurred copies where the digits cannot be read. Your browser then pixelates all of those areas, and only that redacted image goes to the model that reads the name and date of birth. Identity numbers you type yourself are kept in your browser and are never sent to the assistant. A real product should not show the original card to any outside model.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Is this a real insurance offer
No. NexaSure is a fictional AI-first digital insurance company and this is a demo product built for a hackathon. Nothing here is an offer of insurance, and no real premium is charged.
Source: NexaSure demo notice.

## Can the assistant give medical advice
No. The assistant explains the form, the disclosure rules and the product. For any health question, speak to your doctor. The insurer's underwriters decide medical acceptance after your declarations.
Source: NexaSure assistant policy (not a regulation).
