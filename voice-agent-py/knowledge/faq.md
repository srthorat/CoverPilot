# Frequently asked questions

## Why does the form ask for my annual income
Income determines the maximum cover an insurer can offer, usually ten to fifteen times gross annual income. Proof can be a salary slip, Form 16 or an income tax return.

## Why does the form ask for my date of birth and gender
Premiums are priced by age and gender at entry. The rate is then locked for the full thirty-year term, so an exact date of birth matters.

## What happens if I miss a premium
A grace period of thirty days (fifteen for monthly mode) keeps the policy fully in force. After that the policy lapses but can be revived within five years.

## How are claims filed
Digitally, in about two hours, with no physical paperwork. The nominee uploads the death certificate and identity proof; the claim settlement ratio is 99.1 percent.

## Can I change my nominee later
Yes, nominees can be changed any time unless the policy is under the MWPA trust, which is irrevocable.

## Can I increase my cover later
The life stage escalator increases cover automatically on milestones with no medical tests; otherwise a new proposal with fresh underwriting is needed.

## What is co-browsing and what does the agent see
The agent sees the live form state and a reduced-size snapshot of the form when a step changes. Aadhaar, PAN and mobile numbers are never sent; identity documents are masked on the customer's device before being read.
