# UNVERIFIED (the passages say "confirm with the insurer"):
# Whether a level term premium is guaranteed for the whole term: no IRDAI rule found; depends on the product.
# Waiting and survival periods on riders: set by the product, no single IRDAI rule found.
# What happens to riders when the base policy lapses, and whether a pure term plan has a reduced paid-up option: depend on the policy terms; no single rule found.
# The three-year revival window in the life products circular (para 40) is written for unit-linked policies; the revival period for a pure term plan is whatever the policy document states (PPHI circular, section III para 3). Some secondary sites say three years applies to most life products; the circular text read does not say that.
# Whether a life claim rejection letter must cite the specific policy terms: the circular states this expressly for health claims; for life claims only the legally tenable evidence rule, the Section 45 written grounds and the ombudsman contact in grievance replies were found.
# The plain-language glossary entries on indemnity and material fact are general insurance principles, not quotes from a regulation.
# The fifty lakh ombudsman limit was not read in the Gazette notification itself.
