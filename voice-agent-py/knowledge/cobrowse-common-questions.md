# Common questions during the application

## Why is the form asking me this question
Every question has a reason: your age and gender price the premium, income shows what cover is reasonable, the tobacco and health questions let the insurer assess risk, and your identity records satisfy know-your-customer rules. Ask me about any field and I will say why it is there and what to enter, but I only explain the form and never advise on health.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, life section, proposal form para; NexaSure app behaviour.

## What if I do not know my exact income
You do not need an exact figure. Pick the income range your yearly income before tax falls in, using your salary slip, Form 16 or last tax return to help. The insurer may ask for proof later, so choose a range you can support with documents.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Should I take any riders
Riders are optional extras that add cost, so the choice is yours. Critical illness pays a lump sum on diagnosis of a listed illness, accidental death and disability pays after an accident, waiver of premium keeps your cover going if you are disabled by an accident, and hospital cash pays a fixed amount for each hospital day. The suggested critical illness tier is Gold. I can explain each one, but the form requires you to either pick a rider or tick that you want none.
Source: NexaSure demo product terms (not a regulation).

## What is the difference between the three covers
All three covers are the same pure term plan for thirty years and differ only in the sum assured your nominees receive and the monthly price. One crore is seven hundred ninety rupees a month, one and a half crore is one thousand fifty, and two crore is one thousand three hundred eighty, before riders. Nothing else changes between them.
Source: NexaSure demo product terms (not a regulation).

## Where my details go during the application
The assistant sees your live answers such as name, date of birth and income, and a small picture of the form when you change step. It never sees the Aadhaar, PAN or mobile numbers you type, which stay in your browser. In the demo only sample documents are used, and the original of a document stays in your browser; blurred copies and the redacted image go to Meta's models, so the name, date of birth, address and PIN on the redacted image do too.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Can you sign or pay for me
No. Only you can tick the declarations, sign and pay, because those are your decisions and your legal consent. I can take you to step five, explain the process and point at the cards, but I never click sign or pay. The payment in this demo takes no real money.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## What do Confirm and Undo do
When I fill in your income, smoking status or nominees, a card shows what I entered with Confirm and Undo buttons. Confirm keeps it, and Undo restores what was there before. A spoken yes does not count, because speech recognition can make mistakes, so please press the button. You cannot move on until each card is answered.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## What does the card that says a document differs mean
It means a scanned document and what you typed disagree, for example a different spelling of your name. The app shows both values and asks you to choose Keep mine or Use the document's. It never overwrites your answer on its own, and the step stays blocked until you choose.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Why can I not go to the next step
A step opens only when everything before it is complete and valid. The yellow box at the top lists exactly what is missing, such as a PIN code, an identity number, a rider choice or a nominee share. Fix the items and try again, or ask me what is missing and I will tell you one item at a time. Going back to an earlier step is always allowed.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Can I go back and change an earlier answer
Yes. You can use the step tabs or the back buttons to return to any earlier step and change an answer. If you change cover, riders or nominees after signing, the signature no longer counts and you must sign again.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## How do I pause and come back later
Press Pause in the header, or ask me to pause. Your progress is saved in this browser under your proposal code and the voice session ends. Later press Resume and choose the proposal or enter its code. Your Aadhaar, PAN, mobile, signature and payment are not saved, so you will enter them again.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Can I use Hindi or Marathi
Yes. Choose English, Hindi or Marathi at the top, and you can also just speak in Hindi, Marathi or English and I will answer in the language you used. If a word is unclear, ask me to explain it in simple words.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## What if the voice does not hear me or I cannot speak
Type your question in the box at the bottom of the chat and press Ask, which goes through the same assistant as voice. If the connection drops the page tries to reconnect, and you can press Start a session to talk again. Check that the browser has permission to use the microphone.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## How do I stop the agent moving on my screen
Press Revoke Control to take the screen back at once, or press Escape. Pause sharing stops the assistant seeing your screen picture. Control and sharing can be given back with Grant Control and Resume sharing.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## What is Integrity Guardian and how is it different from you
I am the co-browsing agent: I explain each question and fill the application with you. Integrity Guardian is a separate agent that reviews a proposed policy document independently, explains it with page references and flags possible issues, and it never changes your application. Open it from the Integrity Guardian tab or from step five.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Does the critical illness rider use the official illness definitions
I cannot tell you that for certain. IRDAI's 2020 health circular fixed standard wording for twenty-two critical illnesses and said insurers must use it wherever a product covers them, but a later 2024 health circular lists that circular as superseded without repeating the definitions, and I could not find a current IRDAI text that settles whether a rider on a term plan must use that wording. In this demo the illness counts and the waiting and survival periods are NexaSure demo terms. For a real rider, read its definitions and ask the insurer which wording it uses. After you get a proposed policy, the Integrity Guardian can read the rider pages for you.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Standardization of Health Insurance Products, IRDAI/HLT/REG/CIR/193/07/2020, Chapter II (22 critical illnesses, definitions without exception); IRDAI Master Circular on Health Insurance Business, IRDAI/HLT/CIR/PRO/84/5/2024, 29 May 2024, Annexure-6 (supersession); NexaSure demo product terms for the counts and periods.
