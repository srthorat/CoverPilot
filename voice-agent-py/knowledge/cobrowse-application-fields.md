# The NexaSure application, field by field

## Step one: how to write your full legal name in the form
How to write your name: type it exactly as printed on your PAN or Aadhaar card, without a nickname, so the application and your documents match. If a scanned document shows a different spelling, the app does not overwrite what you typed: it shows both and asks which one is right. You can simply say your name and the assistant will type it.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Step one: date of birth format and age limits
Say or type your date of birth as day, month, year. For example, the fifth of March nineteen ninety-two is kept as 05/03/1992. In this demo product you must be between eighteen and sixty-five years old. The date has to be a real calendar date, so a month of fifteen is refused. Your age proof, such as Aadhaar or PAN, has to show the same date.
Source: age limits are NexaSure demo product limits (not a regulation); age proof: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, life section, KYC para.

## Step one: gender field
Choose Male or Female. It is used together with your age to price the premium and has to match your identity documents. Tell the assistant and it will select it for you.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Step one: annual income range options
Income is a dropdown, not a typed number. The ranges are five to ten lakh, ten to fifteen lakh, fifteen to twenty lakh, twenty to thirty-five lakh, and above thirty-five lakh rupees a year before tax. Pick the range your gross yearly income falls in. If you say an exact figure, the assistant picks the matching range. If you are close to the line between two ranges, choose the one your documents support.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Income proof: which documents prove your salary
The insurer may ask you to prove your income and salary with documents. Salaried people usually show recent salary slips, Form 16 or income tax returns, and self-employed people usually show income tax returns and bank statements. The exact list and the number of months differ between insurers and no single official list was found, so check with the insurer. In this demo the sample salary slip button reads the income from a fictional slip.
Source: insurer guidance, for example HDFC Life and Axis Max Life term insurance document pages (practice varies by insurer, not an IRDAI list); sample slip: NexaSure app behaviour.

## Step one: the tobacco question and what counts
Answer yes if you have used tobacco or nicotine, such as cigarettes, bidis, gutkha, cigars or nicotine gum, in the last twelve months, however often. In this demo the twenty percent non-smoker discount applies only when the answer is no. Other insurers may look back twelve to twenty-four months, so the period can differ elsewhere. Answer truthfully, because a wrong answer can cause trouble at claim time.
Source: twelve months is the NexaSure demo term; other insurers vary (Aditya Birla Sun Life Insurance and HDFC Life term insurance pages); no single IRDAI definition was found.

## Step one: city and PIN code
Enter the city you live in and the six-digit PIN code of your address. The PIN has to be exactly six digits. The assistant can fill both from what you say; say the PIN digit by digit, for example four one one zero three eight.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Step one: Aadhaar, PAN and mobile identity records
The identity records box at the bottom of step one needs your twelve-digit Aadhaar number, your PAN in the form five letters, four digits and one letter, and your ten-digit mobile number starting with six, seven, eight or nine. You type these yourself or scan a sample document. The assistant cannot see or fill them, so please never say the numbers aloud. They stay in your browser, show masked unless you are typing in the box, and are left out of what the assistant is sent.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation); the need for identity checks: IRDAI Master Guidelines on AML/CFT, 1 Aug 2022.

## Why PAN is asked and when it is compulsory
PAN has to be quoted when you pay more than fifty thousand rupees in life insurance premium in a financial year, otherwise a Form 60 declaration is used. This demo asks for PAN in every case so the form is complete. The income-tax rules were renumbered from 1 April 2026, so confirm the current rule with the insurer.
Source: Income-tax Rule 114B list of transactions requiring PAN, https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in; the 2026 renumbering was seen in one source only, check with the insurer.

## Sample documents versus typing it yourself
The sample card and salary slip buttons read a fictional document and fill your name, date of birth, PIN and income only where the box is empty. They never replace something you already typed: if the document disagrees you see a card asking whether to keep yours or use the document's. A sample's ID number is filled in for you, but only for the sample.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Step two: choosing the cover amount and monthly price
Three covers are offered for a thirty-year term: one crore rupees at seven hundred ninety rupees a month, one and a half crore at one thousand fifty rupees a month, and two crore at one thousand three hundred eighty rupees a month. These prices are before riders, and there is no GST on individual life cover. Tell the assistant a cover and it selects it, and the premium summary updates at once. The right amount depends on your income, loans and family needs, and the form does not decide it for you.
Source: NexaSure demo product terms (not a regulation); GST exemption: GST Council 56th meeting press release, 3 Sep 2025.

## Step two: the critical illness rider card
The critical illness card has three tiers. Silver pays ten lakh rupees at eighty-nine rupees a month, Gold pays twenty-five lakh at one hundred forty-nine, and Platinum pays fifty lakh at two hundred sixty-nine. Gold is the suggested tier. Picking a tier switches the rider on, and unticking the card removes it. The payout is a lump sum on diagnosis of a listed illness, after a ninety-day waiting period and a fourteen-day survival period in this demo.
Source: NexaSure demo product terms (not a regulation).

## Step two: accidental death and disability options
Option A pays fifty lakh rupees as a lump sum for seventy-nine rupees a month. Option B pays one lakh rupees a month for one hundred months, to replace lost income, for one hundred twenty-nine rupees a month. Choosing either option adds the rider.
Source: NexaSure demo product terms (not a regulation).

## Step two: the waiver of premium rider
The waiver of premium rider costs forty-two rupees a month. If you become disabled by an accident in a way the rider covers, your future premiums for the thirty-year term are waived and your cover stays active.
Source: NexaSure demo product terms (not a regulation).

## Step two: the hospital daily cash rider
The hospital daily cash rider costs fifty-five rupees a month. In the NexaSure demo terms it pays two thousand rupees for each day you are admitted to hospital, whatever the bill. If the card on your screen shows a different daily amount, the card on your screen is what your proposal uses, so read that one out.
Source: NexaSure demo product terms (not a regulation).

## Step two: the life stage cover escalator
The life stage escalator costs forty-nine rupees a month and is a tick box under the riders. Cover rises ten percent a year, or fifty percent on marriage and twenty-five percent on the birth of a child, without a new medical test.
Source: NexaSure demo product terms (not a regulation).

## Step two: you must choose riders or choose none
Before moving on you must either pick at least one rider or tick I do not want any riders, which means base cover only. If you tell the assistant you want none, it ticks that box for you. Choosing a rider afterwards unticks it automatically.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Step two: terminal illness early payout is built in
The terminal illness early payout is included in every cover at no extra cost, so there is nothing to select. The full sum assured can be paid early on diagnosis of a terminal illness with under six months of life expectancy.
Source: NexaSure demo product terms (not a regulation).

## Step three: naming nominees and their shares
Add a primary nominee with a name, a relation and a share. You can have up to four nominees in total: the primary, a second, and two more. The shares must add up to exactly one hundred percent. You can ask the assistant to split the shares equally and it will read the split back to you.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation); nomination rules: Insurance Act 1938 section 39, https://lawgist.in/insurance-act/39

## Step three: a nominee under eighteen needs an appointee
If any nominee is under eighteen, name an adult appointee, such as a grandparent, who receives the money on the child's behalf while the child is a minor. The form will not move on until the appointee is named. Section 39 of the Insurance Act lets the policyholder appoint such a person.
Source: Insurance Act 1938 section 39, https://lawgist.in/insurance-act/39 and https://indiankanoon.org/doc/610691/

## Step three: the Married Women's Property Act trust switch
The trust switch is for a husband insuring his own life for his wife and children under section 6 of the Married Women's Property Act 1874. When it is on, the policy is held in trust for them, the money is generally protected from his creditors, and the beneficiaries cannot be changed later. It only suits wife and children as beneficiaries, so ask a lawyer if you are unsure.
Source: Married Women's Property Act 1874 section 6 (secondary summary), consistent with the nomination and trust knowledge in this base.

## Step three: choosing who to name as nominee
You may name anyone you trust. The Insurance Act gives a nominee automatic beneficial title to the money when the nominees are your parents, spouse or children. For other nominees, ask the insurer or a lawyer how the money is treated, because the law may treat them as collecting it for your legal heirs.
Source: Insurance Act 1938 section 39(7) as read in https://lawgist.in/insurance-act/39 and https://indiankanoon.org/doc/610691/ (secondary text; this is a general reading, check with the insurer or a lawyer).

## Step four: the family heart and chronic disease question
This tick box asks whether your father, mother or siblings were diagnosed with a heart attack, bypass surgery or stroke before age sixty. Tick it if the answer is yes. The assistant explains the question only; it cannot say what a family history means for your health or whether it affects acceptance, because underwriting decides after your declarations.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation); medical boundary per the app.

## Step four: the prior hospitalisation or surgery question
This tick box asks about any inpatient treatment, or specialised medicine taken for longer than forty-eight hours, in the past five years. Tick it if the answer is yes. Answer truthfully, because the proposal is the basis of the policy. The assistant explains what the question asks and cannot advise on any medical matter.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation); proposal as the basis of the policy: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, life section.

## Step four: the Section 45 declaration box
Ticking this box says your answers are true and complete. Section 45 of the Insurance Act says that after three years a life policy cannot be questioned on any ground, and within three years it can be questioned only for fraud or for a wrong or hidden fact that matters, with written grounds. The box has to be ticked by you; the assistant never ticks a declaration for you.
Source: Insurance Act 1938 section 45; IRDAI Master Circular on Life Insurance Products 2024, para 6; NexaSure app behaviour for the tick box.

## Step five: the application check card
The application check on step five lists six checks: profile and documents, riders, nominees, declarations, your signature and your payment. A check shows a tick when it is complete and the reason when it is not. The badge reads Checks passed only when every step before it is valid. You make the declarations, sign and pay yourself.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Step five: e-sign and the demo payment
To sign, type your full name exactly as in the form and tick the declaration. The signature is tied to your answers, so if you change cover, riders or nominees afterwards you must sign again. Then the demo payment screen takes no real money. Only you can click sign and pay; the assistant never does it for you.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation); e-signature background: Controller of Certifying Authorities eSign, https://cca.gov.in/eSign.html

## Step five: download the proposed policy and review it
Before you sign you can download the proposed policy as a draft PDF, and you can send it to Integrity Guardian, a separate review agent, for an independent check. The draft is made in your browser from your answers. It is a draft, not an issued policy and not proof of insurance.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Pause and resume: the proposal code
Every proposal has its own unique code that starts with NXP and shows in the header. Pause saves your progress in this browser and ends the voice session. Resume lists proposals saved in this browser, and you can also enter a code. The assistant will say the code slowly in groups if you ask.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).

## Pause and resume: what is not saved
Pause never saves your Aadhaar, PAN, mobile number, e-signature or payment. After resuming you type the identity numbers again and sign again. Your name, answers, cover, riders and nominees are saved.
Source: NexaSure app behaviour (not a regulation).
