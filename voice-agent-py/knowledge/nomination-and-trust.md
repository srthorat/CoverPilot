# Nomination and trust allocation

## Nominee eligibility (IRDAI Section 39)
Spouse, children, parents or siblings can be nominated. Shares across nominees must total exactly one hundred percent. The nominee receives the death benefit directly.

## Minor nominees and appointees
If a nominee is under eighteen, an adult appointee (guardian) must be named to receive the money in trust until the child turns eighteen. Typical appointees are a grandparent or the other parent.

## Married Women's Property Act 1874, Section 6 trust
Taking the policy under the MWPA creates an irrevocable statutory trust solely for the benefit of the policyholder's wife and children. Claim proceeds cannot be attached by any bank creditor, the income tax department, a court decree or an insolvency resolution. Once created, the policyholder cannot change the beneficiaries or assign the policy. Recommended for business owners and anyone with loans.

## Recommended split example
A common recommendation is seventy percent to the spouse and thirty percent to a child with an appointed guardian, combined with the MWPA trust for creditor protection.
