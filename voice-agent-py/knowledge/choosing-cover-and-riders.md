# Choosing cover, term and riders

## How much cover do I need
There is no IRDAI formula. A common rule of thumb is cover that replaces your income for the years your family depends on you, plus loans, minus savings. Many advisers suggest about ten to twenty times annual income. This is a general idea, not advice.
Source: General industry practice; IRDAI sets no cover-to-income formula.

## What term should I pick
Choose a term that lasts until your dependants no longer need your income, usually until retirement age. A longer term costs more but covers you for more years. This demo offers a thirty-year term.
Source: General industry practice; NexaSure demo product terms.

## Term insurance versus savings plans
Pure term insurance pays only if you die in the term and has no maturity benefit. It does not build savings or a surrender value, and has no loans. Savings and endowment plans cost more for the same cover because they also return money. NexaSure Flexi Shield is a pure term product.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Life Insurance Products 2024; NexaSure demo product terms.

## Do I need a rider
A rider adds a benefit for extra premium, such as critical illness, accidental disability or waiver of premium. It costs more, and total rider premium is capped by IRDAI at thirty percent of the base premium. Pick riders for risks you cannot absorb yourself, and read the exclusions.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Life Insurance Products 2024, rider limits; NexaSure rider list is a demo.

## Do I need a medical test
It depends on your age, cover and answers. Higher cover or older age usually triggers a medical examination, and the insurer pays for it. A test result can change the premium or the terms offered. This explains the process, it is not medical advice.
Source: IRDAI Master Circular on Protection of Policyholders' Interests 2024, proposal form.

## Can I buy more than one policy
Yes. There is no limit on the number of life policies. Disclose your existing policies in the form, because cover is judged on your total insurance. Hiding them is a misstatement.
Source: Insurance Act 1938 section 45; general underwriting practice.

## Is the money safe with the insurer
Insurers are licensed and regulated by IRDAI and must meet solvency rules. NexaSure is a fictional demo company, so it holds no real money and no claim settlement ratio is quoted. For real insurers, IRDAI publishes claim data each year.
Source: Insurance Act 1938; IRDAI annual report.
