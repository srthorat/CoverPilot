Terms and conditions (1 of 2)

1. Definitions

"Policyholder" is the person who owns the policy. "Life assured" is the person whose life is covered. "Nominee" is the person named to receive the sum assured. "Policy year" runs from the commencement date.

2. Grace period

A grace period of thirty days from the premium due date is allowed for quarterly, half-yearly and annual premiums, and fifteen days for monthly premiums. No penalty or late fee is charged and cover continues without a break during the grace period. Single premium policies have no grace period.

3. Free-look period

You have thirty days from receiving this policy document to review it. If you do not agree with any term you may return the policy for cancellation, whatever your reason. The refund is the premium paid minus a proportionate risk premium for the period covered, the cost of any medical examination and the stamp duty. It is not always a full refund.

4. Lapse and revival

If a premium is not paid by the end of the grace period the policy lapses and cover stops. A lapsed policy may be revived within the period stated here, which in this demo product is five years from the first unpaid premium, by paying the unpaid premiums with interest. A health declaration or medical examination may be required. IRDAI leaves the revival period to the policy document.

5. Nomination

You may name one or more nominees and the share each receives, which must total one hundred percent. If a nominee is under eighteen an adult appointee must be named. A policy taken under section 6 of the Married Women's Property Act is held in trust for the wife and children, and its beneficiaries cannot then be changed.

6. Assignment

The policy may be assigned under section 38 of the Insurance Act 1938 by written notice to NexaSure. An assignment replaces any earlier nomination.

7. Premium notices and updates

Please keep your mobile number, address and bank details up to date so premium notices and claim payments are not delayed.

Terms and conditions (2 of 2)

8. Suicide

If the life assured dies by suicide within twelve months of the date the risk started, or of a revival, the nominee receives at least eighty percent of the premiums paid or the surrender value, whichever is higher, provided the policy is in force. Death by suicide after twelve months is not excluded for this reason.

9. Section 45 of the Insurance Act 1938

After three years from the latest of the date of issue, the date the risk began, the revival date or the rider date, no life insurance policy can be called in question on any ground. Within those three years it can be questioned only for fraud, or for a wrong or hidden fact that matters to the life expectancy, and the insurer must give its grounds in writing. If a claim is rejected for a misstatement the premiums collected are returned within ninety days.

10. Exclusions

Apart from the suicide provision above, this policy has no general exclusion for the death benefit. Rider benefits carry the limits stated on page 2.

11. Claims procedure

The nominee should inform NexaSure as soon as possible through the portal or customer care. Documents usually needed: the claim form, the original policy document, the death certificate, the nominee's identity and bank details, and further papers if the death needs investigation. By rule a death claim must be settled within fifteen days of intimation, or within forty-five days if it needs investigation. NexaSure aims to respond within two hours; this is a demo target, not an audited result.

12. Tax

Under the old tax regime premiums may qualify for a deduction within the overall limit of 1.5 lakh rupees a year under sections 123 and 126 of the Income-tax Act 2025. A death benefit is generally tax free under section 10(10D). This is general information, not tax advice.
